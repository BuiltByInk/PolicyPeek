## How do I opt out in ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs' privacy policy says you can opt out "by navigating to the 'Data use' menu in the 'Terms and Privacy' section of your ElevenLabs account". ([ElevenLabs](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/elevenlabs.io/))

## How do I opt out in Photoroom?

Photoroom's terms say you can opt out at any time by changing your account settings. ([Photoroom](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/photoroom.com/))

## How do I opt out in HeyGen?

By emailing HeyGen's privacy team; PolicyPeek notes there isn't an account setting. ([HeyGen](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/heygen.com/))

## What if the tool has no opt-out?

For the tools rated **Trains on your data, no opt-out**, the policy gives you no way to stop training. InVideo says you can stop it only by deleting the data or closing your account. If you're uploading client work, music stems or photos of people, consider Moises or Pictory, whose terms say they won't train on your content without your written agreement.

## FAQ

### Does Suno train on my songs?

Suno's privacy policy says it uses submissions and other content 'to train and enhance the models that power our Services'. No training opt-out is stated.

### Does Remove.bg train on my photos?

Remove.bg's privacy policy says it may analyse your uploads to train its algorithms, models and AI products. PolicyPeek found no opt-out.

### Can I stop ElevenLabs training on my voice data?

Yes. ElevenLabs says you can opt out in the 'Data use' menu under 'Terms and Privacy' in your account.

### Which AI music tool doesn't train on my uploads?

Moises' terms say it will not use your content or output to train AI models unless you expressly authorise it.

### Does Midjourney train on my images?

PolicyPeek's Midjourney verdict is under review: its policy doesn't clearly say, and PolicyPeek couldn't re-read the page (docs.midjourney.com blocks automated reads).
