# Do AI Meeting Note-Takers Train on Your Recordings? Zoom, Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv and Granola

*Last checked: 24 October 2026. Verdicts come from each company's own published policy, read by [PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/). Not legal advice.*

Zoom AI Companion, Fireflies.ai and tl;dv say in their own policies that they don't train AI on your meeting content. Granola trains on de-identified data but lets you opt out in account settings. Otter.ai's policy says it trains on de-identified recordings and transcripts, and its help centre describes an opt-out in account settings. ([All tools](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/))

## How do I opt out of Granola training?

Granola's privacy policy says it only uses de-identified data to train AI models, "which you can opt-out of within your Granola account settings". ([Granola](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/granola.ai/))

## Can I opt out of Otter.ai training?

Yes. Otter's [help centre](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/25352082563863-Provide-feedback-to-Otter) says to go to Account Settings > Meetings > Feedback and Training and set Help improve Otter to "Don't allow sharing"; then "Otter will not access your conversation for training and product improvement." Its [privacy policy](https://otter.ai/privacy-policy) separately mentions training on de-identified recordings and doesn't say whether the setting covers that, so the tools above that say they don't train are the safer choice. ([Otter.ai](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/otter.ai/))

## Is Zoom's promise unconditional?

PolicyPeek notes that Zoom's promise covers meeting audio, video, chat, transcripts and AI outputs; usage data and de-identified aggregates are outside it. ([Zoom AI Companion](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/zoom.com/))

## Why this matters for other people in the call

A recording includes everyone in the meeting, not only you. If you record clients or colleagues, a tool that doesn't train is the safer default.

## FAQ

### Does Otter.ai train on my recordings?

Otter's privacy policy says it trains its AI on de-identified audio recordings and transcriptions. Its help centre describes an opt-out under Account Settings > Meetings > Feedback and Training.

### Does Zoom use my meetings to train AI?

Zoom says it doesn't use meeting audio, video, chat, transcripts or AI outputs to train its or third-party AI models. Usage data and de-identified aggregates are outside that promise.

### Does Fireflies train on my data?

No. Fireflies' policy says it doesn't use personal information for AI model training and contractually prohibits its vendors from doing so.

### Does tl;dv train on meeting recordings?

No. tl;dv's privacy policy says it doesn't use customer content, including meeting recordings and transcripts, to train foundation or large language models.

### Can I opt out of Granola training?

Yes. Granola says its de-identified-data training can be switched off in account settings.
