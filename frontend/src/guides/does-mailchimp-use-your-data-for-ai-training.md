# Does Mailchimp Use Your Data for AI Training? Under Review, Plus HubSpot, Ahrefs and Other Marketing Tools

*Last checked: 28 October 2026. Verdicts come from each company's own published policy, read by [PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/). Not legal advice.*

Mailchimp is **under review**. Its terms let it use your data for machine learning, but whether you can opt out of that isn't clearly stated, so PolicyPeek has taken its verdict down while it re-checks. HubSpot and Zapier train by default but let you opt out; Semrush, n8n, Frase and Writesonic say they don't train.

## What does Mailchimp's policy say?

From [Mailchimp's terms](https://mailchimp.com/legal/terms/) (page shows "Updated October 10, 2025"), as re-read by PolicyPeek on 28 October 2026:

> "We may use your Inputs and Outputs, including Customer Data, for machine learning purposes in order to develop and improve the AI Model, the Services, and similar products and features, and you instruct us to process Customer Data for such purposes. You may select preferences about your data by visiting your account settings."

Mailchimp's [account settings help page](https://mailchimp.com/help/set-account-security-options/) describes one data setting, "Data analytics":

> "If you prefer not to share your data, you can opt out of data analytics."

> "To opt out, scroll to the Data analytics setting heading and uncheck the box next to Include my data in Mailchimp's data analytics projects."

That help page doesn't say the setting covers AI or machine learning. Until a Mailchimp source says clearly how to stop training, PolicyPeek won't publish a verdict for it.

## Can I stop HubSpot training on my data?

Yes. HubSpot's own help article says the AI training setting is on by default and Super Admins can opt out at any time, and that the same terms apply to the free CRM. ([HubSpot](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/hubspot.com/))

## FAQ

### Does Mailchimp use my data for AI training?

Its terms say: "We may use your Inputs and Outputs, including Customer Data, for machine learning purposes in order to develop and improve the AI Model, the Services, and similar products and features, and you instruct us to process Customer Data for such purposes. You may select preferences about your data by visiting your account settings." PolicyPeek's verdict is under review.

### Can I opt out of Mailchimp AI training?

Not clearly stated. The terms say "You may select preferences about your data by visiting your account settings." The only setting Mailchimp's help page describes is "Data analytics": "To opt out, scroll to the Data analytics setting heading and uncheck the box next to Include my data in Mailchimp's data analytics projects." That page doesn't mention AI training.

### Does HubSpot train AI on my data?

By default, yes, but HubSpot says Super Admins can opt out of AI model training at any time, including on the free CRM.

### Does Ahrefs train on my data?

Ahrefs' terms say it may use User Content, including content you give its AI tools, to train its machine learning models. No opt-out was found.

### Does Buffer train AI on my posts?

Unclear. Buffer says AI Assistant text is shared with OpenAI but doesn't say whether training happens, so PolicyPeek rates it 'Policy unclear'.
