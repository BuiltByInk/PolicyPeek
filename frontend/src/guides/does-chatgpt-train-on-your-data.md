# Does ChatGPT Train on Your Data? What OpenAI's Own Policy Says

*Last checked: 24 October 2026. Verdicts come from each company's own published policy, read by [PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/). Not legal advice.*

Yes, by default. PolicyPeek gives ChatGPT the badge **Trains by default, you can opt out**. Its page says: "ChatGPT's published policy says it may train on your data, but you can opt out." ([ChatGPT on PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/))

## What OpenAI's policy actually says

PolicyPeek quotes this from the company's own published policy:

> "We may use Content you provide us to improve our Services, for example to train the models that power ChatGPT. Read our instructions on how you can opt out of our use of your Content to train our models."

Source: [ChatGPT on PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/), built from https://openai.com/policies/row-privacy-policy/

## Can you stop ChatGPT training on your chats?

According to the policy, there is an opt-out: the policy itself points readers to OpenAI's instructions on how to opt out of their Content being used for training. ([ChatGPT on PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/))

## How long does ChatGPT keep your data?

The facts PolicyPeek pulled from the policy say: ([ChatGPT on PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/))
- Personal data is kept as long as needed to provide the services, or for legitimate business purposes such as resolving disputes, safety, security and legal compliance.
- Data you delete is removed within 30 days of the deletion request, unless it has to be kept for safety or legal reasons.
- Temporary Chats are deleted automatically within 30 days, with the same exception.
- The policy mentions GDPR.

## Why it's "you can opt out" and not "doesn't train"

PolicyPeek keeps "you can opt out" separate from the green badge on purpose. An opt-out is a real protection, but it's weaker than not training by default, because you have to find the setting and switch it off yourself. ([Methodology](https://getpolicypeek.com/about/))

## FAQ

### Does ChatGPT train on my conversations?

By default, yes. OpenAI's policy says it may use the content you provide to train the models that power ChatGPT. ([ChatGPT](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/))

### Can I opt out of ChatGPT training?

Yes. The policy refers to OpenAI's instructions on how to opt out. ([ChatGPT](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/))

### Are Temporary Chats deleted?

The policy says Temporary Chats are deleted automatically within 30 days, unless they have to be kept for safety or legal reasons. ([ChatGPT](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/))
