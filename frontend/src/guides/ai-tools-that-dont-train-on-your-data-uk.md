# Which AI Tools Don't Train on Your Data? A UK Guide (October 2026)

*Last checked: 24 October 2026. Verdicts come from each company's own published policy, read by [PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/). Not legal advice.*

On 24 Oct 2026, 14 of the 59 AI tools PolicyPeek checked had the badge **Doesn't train on your data**. That means the tool's own published policy says your data isn't used to train AI models, or is used only if you opt in. ([PolicyPeek home](https://getpolicypeek.com/), [All tools](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/))

## Read the small print: scope matters

- **Sourcegraph Cody:** the quoted promise covers Cody Enterprise and Cody Pro teams.
- **Kagi:** the policy says "whenever possible".
- **Zoom:** usage data and de-identified aggregates aren't covered by the promise.
([All tools](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/))

## What's different for UK users?

PolicyPeek gives each tool one verdict, whatever country you're in. The live site has one UK-specific note: for [Bolt.new](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/bolt.new/) (**Trains by default, you can opt out**), "Accounts in the EEA, UK and Switzerland don't need to opt out." ([All tools](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/))

PolicyPeek also explains why an opt-out doesn't get the green badge: "GDPR generally expects opt-in consent, not an opt-out you have to go find in a settings page or a support form." It's not legal advice or a finding about any law. ([Methodology](https://getpolicypeek.com/about/))

## Popular tools that do train by default

[ChatGPT](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/openai.com/), [Claude](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/claude.com/) (consumer plans), [Cursor](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/cursor.com/) and [Grammarly](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/grammarly.com/) are **Trains by default, you can opt out**.
[DeepL](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/deepl.com/) (rated on its free tier), [Otter.ai](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/otter.ai/) and [Perplexity AI](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/perplexity.ai/) are **Trains on your data, no opt-out**. ([All tools](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/))
[Gemini](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/google.com/) has no product verdict yet, because only a company-wide policy was found.

## How PolicyPeek decides

An AI model extracts the facts from the policy, then fixed rules decide the badge. Verdicts come from the company's own published policies. Uncertain results are held for human review, and a good verdict for a tool that's new to the site always waits for a person to check it first. ([Methodology](https://getpolicypeek.com/about/))

## FAQ

### Which AI chatbot doesn't train on your data?

Of the tools PolicyPeek lists, [Kagi](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/kagi.com/) and [You.com](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/you.com/) have **Doesn't train on your data**.

### Is there an AI coding assistant that doesn't train on my code?

[Tabnine](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/tabnine.com/) does, and so does [Sourcegraph Cody](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/sourcegraph.com/) for Enterprise and Pro teams.

### Do UK users have to opt out of Bolt.new training?

PolicyPeek's note says accounts in the EEA, UK and Switzerland don't need to opt out. ([Bolt.new](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/bolt.new/))

### What does "Policy unclear" mean?

It means the policy doesn't clearly say either way. It isn't a middle grade. ([Methodology](https://getpolicypeek.com/about/))
