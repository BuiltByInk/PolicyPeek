# Which AI Tools Don't Train on Your Data? A UK Guide (November 2026)

Of the 61 AI tools PolicyPeek has checked, 15 have published policies that say they don't train AI on your data, including Notion AI, Zoom AI Companion, Fireflies.ai, tl;dv, Tabnine and Kagi. ChatGPT trains by default and Claude trains on consumer plans by default; both let you opt out. DeepL's free tier trains with no opt-out found.

*Last checked: 4 November 2026 (latest check across the tools below; each row shows its own date). Verdicts come from each company's own published policy, read by [PolicyPeek](https://getpolicypeek.com/). Not legal advice.*

## Read the caveats

Several of these promises have limits written into the policy itself. Kagi says it uses providers that don't train "whenever possible". Sourcegraph's clause covers Cody Enterprise and Cody Pro teams. Zoom's promise leaves out usage data and de-identified aggregates. Moises and Pictory allow training where you have expressly agreed. Open each tool page to read the full wording.

## FAQ

### Which AI tools don't train on your data?

Of the 61 tools PolicyPeek has checked, 15 have the badge "Doesn't train on your data", including Notion AI, Zoom AI Companion, Fireflies.ai, tl;dv, Tabnine, Kagi, Writesonic and You.com. Each badge comes from the tool's own published policy.

### Does ChatGPT train on your data?

By default, yes, but you can opt out. OpenAI's policy says: "We may use Content you provide us to improve our Services, for example to train the models that power ChatGPT." It links instructions for opting out.

### Does Claude train on your data?

On consumer plans (Free, Pro, Max), Claude trains by default unless you opt out in account settings. PolicyPeek recorded that commercial plans don't train by default.

### Is "doesn't train" the same as "private"?

No. The badge only covers whether the policy allows AI training on your data. It says nothing about storage, sharing or security, and it isn't a finding about any law. Not legal advice.

### Do UK users have to opt out of Bolt.new training?

PolicyPeek's note says accounts in the EEA, UK and Switzerland don't need to opt out. ([Bolt.new](https://getpolicypeek.com/tools/bolt.new/))
