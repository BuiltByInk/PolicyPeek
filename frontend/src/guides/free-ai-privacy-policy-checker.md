## How is it different from reading the policy yourself?

It does the same thing, faster: finds the training clause, checks whether an opt-out is real (deleting your account, or "data preferences", doesn't count), and notes plan differences such as free versus enterprise. Every verdict links the exact page used and the date it was checked, so you can verify it.

## How accurate is it?

Verdicts come only from the company's own published text. An AI model extracts the facts and fixed rules decide the badge; uncertain results are held for human review, and a new "good" verdict always waits for a person. ## FAQ

### Is there a free tool to check if an AI app trains on my data?

Yes. PolicyPeek (getpolicypeek.com) is free and shows a plain verdict for 49 AI tools, based on their own policies.

### Where does PolicyPeek get its information?

From each company's own published privacy policy, terms or data-use page. Each tool page links the source and the date checked.

### Does PolicyPeek count deleting your account as an opt-out?

No. Only a way to stop training while still using the service counts.

### Is PolicyPeek legal advice?

No. It summarises what companies publish; check the source and your own contract for important decisions.
