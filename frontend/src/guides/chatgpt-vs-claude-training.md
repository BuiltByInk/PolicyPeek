# ChatGPT vs Claude: Which Uses Your Chats for Training?

Both ChatGPT and Claude can use your chats for training by default, and both let you opt out. OpenAI's policy says it may use your content to train the models that power ChatGPT. Anthropic's says it may train on consumer Free, Pro and Max chats unless you opt out in settings.

## FAQ

### Does ChatGPT use my chats for training?

By default, yes. OpenAI's policy says it may use content you provide "to train the models that power ChatGPT", and it links instructions for opting out.

### Does Claude use my chats for training?

On consumer plans (Free, Pro, Max), yes by default: Anthropic "may use your Inputs and Outputs to train and improve Anthropic AI models, unless you opt out through your account settings." Commercial plans don't train by default.

### Is Claude more private than ChatGPT?

On training, PolicyPeek gives both the same badge: Trains by default, you can opt out. The badge doesn't measure other privacy questions. Not legal advice.

### How do I stop ChatGPT or Claude training on my chats?

Both policies describe an opt-out. ChatGPT's policy links OpenAI's opt-out instructions; Claude's says to opt out through your account settings.
