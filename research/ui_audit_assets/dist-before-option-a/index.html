How a rating gets made

  1. We pull facts from the tool's own published privacy policy. We don't guess.
  2. One fixed rule, the same for every tool, turns those facts into a verdict. It isn't an AI's opinion.
  3. Clear-cut results publish automatically. Anything uncertain gets a person's eyes first.
